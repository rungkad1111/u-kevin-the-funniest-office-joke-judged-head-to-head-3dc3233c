One short line about office life is the whole entry. A judge model reads entries two at a time against a published brief and keeps the funnier one — the line that makes it laugh hardest takes the purse.

## The entry

> I replied-all asking everyone to stop replying all — 400 people agreed, one email at a time.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt`, after a runtime gate check that it is 1–200 characters and not only whitespace — the exact gate the sealed scorer applies.

```sh
node main.ts
```

## Competition

[The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-funniest-office-joke-judged-head-to-head-3dc3233c) on make.fun — $2 pot, head-to-head judging, 3 Swiss rounds plus a playoff among the top 12.
